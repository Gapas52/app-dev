# My Favorite Movie: John Wick

> "People keep asking if I'm back and I haven't really had an answer, but yeah, I'm thinking I'm back."

---

## 🎬 Overview
**John Wick** is a 2014 action thriller directed by *Chad Stahelski*. It stars **Keanu Reeves** as a retired hitman seeking vengeance after a mobster steals his car and kills his puppy.

---

## 🔑 Highlights
* **Genre:** Action / Thriller
* **Lead Actor:** Keanu Reeves
* **Release Year:** 2014

### Why I Love This Movie:
1. **Gun-Fu Choreography:** Incredible, realistic action sequences.
2. **World-Building:** The mysterious assassin underworld and the *Continental Hotel*.
3. **Pacing:** Fast-paced plot with minimal fluff.
